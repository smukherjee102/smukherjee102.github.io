Shourish Mukherjee

Biochemistry📚

My journey into the world of biology began quite early, sparked in second grade by The Human Body Book by Steve Parker, which opened my eyes to anatomy, physiology, and the complex mechanisms of disease. What has kept me fascinated with the biological sciences ever since is the sheer magnitude of what remains undiscovered, coupled with how deeply applicable science is to everyday life on a personal and global scale. Currently, I am pursuing my undergraduate studies as a Biochemistry major at the University of California, Riverside, maintaining a rigorous focus on the sciences. My academic path is driven by a deep-seated desire to explore the microscopic basis of diseases and understand how additive environmental and genetic factors influence human health. Over the years, I have complemented my formal coursework—spanning biochemistry, organic chemistry, genetics, and physical chemistry—with specialized online training and advanced foundational courses, including bioinformatics and data science in precision medicine from Stanford Medicine.



Beyond the classroom, I have sought out hands-on research and clinical experiences to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application. As a laboratory intern in Dr. Bahman Anvari’s Biophotonics Lab at UC Riverside, I gained valuable wet-lab experience utilizing cell culturing techniques, operating fluorescence microscopy, and conducting spectrometer data analysis. My research interests also intersect with computational biology and translational medicine; previously, I interned at Cellworks, where I researched drug mechanisms of action and compiled clinical data to support AI-driven models for personalized cancer drug regimens. Additionally, my passion for healthcare innovation has led me to contribute written insights as an intern for the Stanford Healthcare Innovation Lab, exploring topics ranging from future medical technologies to preventive care.My commitment to health and community extends beyond the laboratory. I actively engage in public health and campus leadership as a student representative on the Integrated Pesticide Management Committee through the UCR Office of Sustainability, where I also lead awareness campaigns promoting campus health initiatives. Clinically, I have served as a COPE Health Scholar at Kaiser Permanente in Riverside, assisting patient care delivery teams across departments such as medical telemetry, radiology, and cardiology. Furthermore, I contribute my time as a volunteer at local food banks, helping support community welfare and food safety standards.
